Getting collabs is the fun part. Managing them isn't.

Most creators run collabs from DMs, email threads and a spreadsheet. Here's what that costs.

Endless emails

Rate cards, quotes, follow-ups and "just checking in" mails, written by hand every time.

Draining negotiations

Low-budget and barter offers eat hours, and you second-guess every counter-offer.

Calls that go nowhere

Meetings get booked before the other side has even agreed to your price.

Payments slip

Balances get forgotten, and chasing people for money feels awkward.

How a collab runs on Hustlo

From the first message to the next repeat collab, every step is tracked and the repetitive parts happen automatically.

  1. Collab request

    Brands, businesses or other creators fill your collab form, linked from your bio or email.

  2. Screened

    Your rules check budget, deliverables and timeline.

  3. Quote & negotiate

    Your rates go out in a branded quote. Counter-offers follow your rules.

  4. Meeting

    A call link is offered only after your terms are accepted.

  5. Create & go live

    Track deliverables, approvals and go-live dates.

  6. Get paid & rebook

    Automatic reminders, then offers that bring clients back.

Questions

Who is Hustlo for?

Creators of any niche who take paid collaborations, whether that's brand campaigns, featuring or promoting other creators' work, or paid shoutouts. Talent managers handling several creators can use it too.

Does Hustlo find collabs for me?

No. Hustlo isn't a marketplace. It manages the collabs that come to you, so you can say yes to more of them without drowning in admin.

Will Hustlo negotiate or send emails without me?

Only within the rules you set. Your rates, minimum budget and templates decide what goes out automatically, and anything outside them waits for your approval.

Do brands or clients need to sign up?

No. They fill your collab form and open quotes through a simple link. Only you need an account.

Does Hustlo collect payments for me?

Payments go directly to you. Hustlo tracks what's been paid and what's due, and sends reminders. Payment-link integrations are planned.

Do I need any technical skills?

No. Pick your niche, add your rates, and Hustlo suggests email templates and a starting setup you can adjust anytime.
