Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy explains what information Hustlo ("we", "us") collects through hustlo.in and our early-access waitlist, why we collect it, and what you can do about it. Hustlo is currently in early development, and this policy covers the website and waitlist only. We will update it before the Hustlo product launches.

What we collect

When you join the waitlist, we collect:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your Instagram handle
  • What you create, and roughly how many paid collaboration enquiries you get each month

Our hosting provider may also record basic technical information when you visit the site, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you view. This is used to keep the site running and secure.

How we use it

  • To contact you about Hustlo early access and product updates
  • To invite you, if you're interested, to share feedback on how you manage collaborations
  • To understand which creators Hustlo should be built for first

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers to run the website and waitlist: website hosting (Vercel), form handling (Formspree) and email. They process your information only to provide these services to us. They may store data on servers outside India.

How long we keep it

We keep waitlist information until Hustlo launches and early access is complete, or until you ask us to delete it, whichever comes first.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to:

  • See the information we hold about you
  • Correct it
  • Delete it and remove you from the waitlist
  • Stop sending you emails

Every email we send includes a way to opt out. For anything else, email us at admin@hustlo.in, and we'll respond within a reasonable time.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. If the changes are significant, we'll tell waitlist members by email.

Contact

Questions about privacy: admin@hustlo.in
