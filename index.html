Questions

Who is Hustlo for?

Independent creators who get paid collaboration requests, such as book reviewers, poets, voiceover artists and influencers, plus small publishers and agencies.

Do I need any technical skills?

No. You add your services, prices and policies through a guided setup, and Hustlo handles the rest. No spreadsheets, code or workflow tools.

Will it work with my own prices and services?

Yes. Everything is configurable: services, packages, client categories, advance percentages, payment terms and message templates.

Does Hustlo collect payments for me?

Payments go directly to you. Hustlo tracks what's been paid and what's due, and sends reminders. Payment-link integrations are planned.

How much will it cost?

Pricing will be announced before launch. Waitlist members will hear about it first.
